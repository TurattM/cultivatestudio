The chef behind the studio

Marco Turatti

The menu is the last step. The farm, the fisher and the fermentation jar are where the work begins.

Italian-born, trained in Venice. Sixteen years opening and leading luxury hotel kitchens across Europe, the Middle East and Asia, from starred dining rooms in Palma, Paris and Rome to the first W in Europe and Singapore’s Publico.

Today, Culinary Director of InterContinental Phuket Resort: more than 120 chefs, a farmer network across Southern Thailand, a resort farm and a fermentation lab.

Ask Cultivate

What is in season in Southern Thailand in October, and what pairs with torch ginger?

October: mangosteen, rambutan, sator, pak wan pa and the first wild pepper. Torch ginger sits well with acid and sea — try it with your kung tamari, lime leaf and raw scallop. Your plum ferment would echo its sourness.

Most AI searches the internet. Cultivate does not. It answers from two sources only:
